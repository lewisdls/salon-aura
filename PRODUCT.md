# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Women in Santo Domingo Este, Dominican Republic looking for hair and beauty
services (cuts, color, extensions, treatments, nails, lashes). They arrive on
mobile most of the time, often from Instagram or a WhatsApp referral, wanting to
gauge whether the salon feels trustworthy and cared-for before booking a first
appointment. The job to be done: feel confident this is the place, then book a
cita with as little friction as possible.

## Product Purpose
Salon Aura's marketing site exists to convert local interest into booked
appointments and to communicate that the salon is a personal, welcoming refuge
where a client's individual style is the focus. Success = a visitor who feels
warmly received and books (or messages) without hesitation.

## Brand Personality
Warm, inviting, personal — a neighborhood beauty refuge, not a cold luxury chain.
Confident and well-kept, but the emotional register is care and belonging.
Spanish-language, feminine, generous. The site should feel like being welcomed
in, not sold to.

## Anti-references
- Cold, clinical high-end spa minimalism (all whitespace, no warmth).
- Generic AI landing-page scaffolding: uppercase tracked eyebrows on every
  section, numbered section markers, identical icon-card grids, hero-metric
  templates, gradient text.
- Corporate SaaS gloss. This is a human, local, hands-on business.
- Editorial-magazine cosplay (display-serif + drop caps + broadsheet grid) that
  would read as pretentious for a neighborhood salon.

## Design Principles
- **Warmth over polish.** Every choice should make the visitor feel welcomed and
  cared for, not impressed by slickness.
- **One clear path to booking.** The cita is the point; it should always be
  within reach without shouting on every line.
- **Show the real salon.** Lead with the actual people, spaces, and work — the
  existing photography is the proof, not decoration.
- **Rhythm, not uniformity.** Vary section composition and spacing so the page
  breathes and tells a story instead of stacking equal blocks.
- **Mobile is the main stage.** Most visitors are on a phone from Instagram;
  the phone layout is the design, not an afterthought.

## Anti-references (imagery & scope)
Redesign works within the existing warm palette (oxblood `#9E2B2A`, cream
`#FAF5F1`, rose/beige neutrals, near-black `#121212`), the Outfit typeface, and
the salon's existing photography. Ambition is a bold reimagining of layout,
hierarchy, and motion — not new brand colors, fonts, or stock imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. The oxblood-on-cream and
  cream-on-oxblood pairings must be verified; avoid light-gray body text on
  tinted backgrounds.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all entrance and scroll motion.
- Spanish (es) as the document language; keep copy legible and generous in size
  for a broad age range of clients.
